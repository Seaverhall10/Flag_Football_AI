Home Command

60-Minute Practice Station Cards · Cy-Fair K/1 Lions

Clip onto station cones to guide assistant coaches and parent helpers.

0–8m
Station 1: Arrival & Equipment Setup

Flags and belts on properly (check clips). Set cones on all 6 landmark holes (C-RG, C-LG, RG-RT, LG-LT, RT Out, LT Out). No player names on boards.

8–15m
Station 2: Warm Movement & Fit-Up

Line movement cues: Find jersey. Head out. Hands inside. Light tag drills, agility footwork, no live tackling.

15–25m
Station 3: Shotgun Snap Station

Shotgun ~3 yards direct to designated runner only. Focus on steady stance, two-hand exchange, and firm tuck into chest.

25–40m
Station 4: 5v4 Scripted Drill (18 Reps)

30-second next-ball cadence! 5 offense vs 4 look-team defense. Focus: Snap, assignments, landmark, legal contact. Tick tracker.

40–52m
Station 5: Six-Run Cycle

One color at a time across all 6 runs. Landmark cone is the target hole. Lead attacks hole first, blocks LB. Runner cuts north.

52–58m
Station 6: Front & Cornerback Defense

Front/DL: Stay home, then flag. LB fill. CB: Nothing outside.

58–60m
Station 7: Close & Team Review

Hydration, repeat 5 cues together, record clean reps on tracker clipboard, remind parents of next date on calendar.

Print on cardstock or laminate for station cones.
