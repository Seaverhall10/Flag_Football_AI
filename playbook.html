Direct snap to designated runner (~3 yards). Lead attacks hole first and blocks linebacker. Runner follows, plants, and explodes north.

Assignments

Offense (8)

  • C: Clean 3-yard snap, then drive DL.
  • LG / RG: Find your jersey. Head out. Hands inside.
  • LT / RT: Seal edge.
  • WR: Stalk or clear CB.
  • Lead: Hole first. LB.
  • Runner: Follow. Plant. Go.

Look defense (4)

  • Front: Stay home, then flag.
  • CB: Nothing outside.
