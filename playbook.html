Execution Key: Direct snap to designated runner (~3 yards). Lead attacks hole first and blocks linebacker. Runner follows, plants, and explodes north.

Position Assignment Matrix

Offense (8 Players)

  • Center (C): Clean 3-yard snap direct to runner, then drive DL.
  • Guards (LG / RG): Find jersey, head out, hands inside, maintain gap.
  • Tackles (LT / RT): Seal edge, do not allow defenders inside.
  • Wide Receiver (WR): Stalk block or clear out cornerback.
  • Lead Blocker (L): Attack the called hole landmark FIRST, pick up LB.
  • Designated Runner (R): Catch cleanly. Follow. Plant. Go.

Defense (Look Team 4)

  • Front 3 (DL / LB): Stay home, fill gaps, then pull flag. No lunging.
  • Cornerback (CB): Nothing outside. Keep outside arm free, force runner back inside.
