Game Day Routine & Culture

Pregame Routine (Arrive 20 Mins Early)

  • Flags & Belts: Clipped tightly, no loose straps.
  • Shotgun Snap Warmup: 10 crisp 3-yard snaps to each designated runner.
  • Call-and-Response: Review the 5 cues with the entire squad.

Sideline Discipline

  • One Adult Voice: Coaches call the plays clearly; parents cheer effort!
  • Stick to the Six Runs: No new unpracticed plays on game day.
  • Hydration: Water breaks after every quarter.
