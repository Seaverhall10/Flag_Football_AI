Game Day Sideline Protocols

Pregame Routine (20 Mins Prior)

  • Flags Checked: Belts tightened, clips verified.
  • Direct Snap Warmup: 10 crisp shotgun snaps to each runner.
  • Review 5 Cues: Call response with entire unit.

Sideline Discipline

  • One Coach Calling: Clear, loud color + symbol calls.
  • Cheer Cues: Encourage effort, landmark discipline, and teamwork.
  • No Scheme Adjustments: Stick to the six locked runs.
