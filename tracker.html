Target: 5-of-6 Clean Reps (Snap, Assignments, Landmark, Legal Contact) across 2 consecutive practices before installing new plays.

What Constitutes a "Clean Rep"?

4-Point Clean Checklist

  • 1. Snap: Crisp, direct shotgun snap ~3 yards into designated runner's chest.
  • 2. Assignment: OL finds jersey, head out, hands inside; Lead attacks hole first.
  • 3. Landmark: Runner follows lead, plants at designated cone, cuts north.
  • 4. Legal Contact: Hands inside, no holding, no flag guarding, no face contact.

Coaching Rule

During the 18-rep drill, move at a 30-second next-ball pace. Do not stop the session to chase a pretty rep — mark the tracker, cue the player with 3 words, and snap the next ball!
