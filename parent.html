Parent sheet · Cy-Fair K/1 Lions

Ages 5–6. 8-on-8 CFSA flag. Practice and game times come from the coach calendar (see the schedule block on the team page).

What kids hear: Find your jersey. Head out. Hands inside. Hole first. Follow. Plant. Go. Stay home, then flag. Nothing outside.

What we do not do: New plays. Player names on public pages. Live tackling.

Gear: Cleats or turf shoes, water, flags if the club does not issue a spare.

Sideline: Cheer the cues, not a new scheme. One adult voice on the field.

The six colors (so home practice matches us)

  • Red diamond — inside right — C-RG
  • Blue circle — inside left — C-LG
  • Gold star — off-tackle right — RG-RT
  • Green triangle — off-tackle left — LG-LT
  • Orange square — wide right — RT outside hip
  • Purple hexagon — wide left — LT outside hip

Print this page. Colors may be on or off; symbols still read.
