CFSA 8-on-8 Flag Football · Ages 5–6 · Team Guide & Sideline Expectations

Welcome to the Lions!

Our focus in the K/1 division is building confidence, fundamental athletic skills, discipline, and fun. We teach a simple, structured 6-run system with consistent cues so every child learns their assignment without confusion.

What Kids Hear on the Field

We use simple, consistent 3-word cues:

  • Line (OL): Find your jersey. Head out. Hands inside.
  • Lead Blocker: Hole first. Then Linebacker.
  • Runner: Follow. Plant. Go.
  • Defense: Stay home, then flag. Nothing outside.

Home Practice Color Language

If you're practicing with your player in the backyard, you can use our 6 team colors:

♦ Red Diamond: Inside Right (run to the right of Center)

● Blue Circle: Inside Left (run to the left of Center)

★ Gold Star: Off-Tackle Right (run between Guard & Tackle)

▲ Green Triangle: Off-Tackle Left (run between Guard & Tackle)

■ Orange Square: Wide Right (sweep outside to right sideline)

⬡ Purple Hexagon: Wide Left (sweep outside to left sideline)

Sideline Culture & Parent Role

  • One Voice on the Field: Coaches call the cues from the sideline so players can focus without split instructions.
  • Cheer the Effort: Cheer hustle, good flag pulls, and positive team spirit!
  • Required Gear: Youth molded cleats (or sneakers), water bottle, mouthguard (recommended), and flag belt.
  • Schedule & Updates: Access the team web app anytime: seaverhall10.github.io/Flag_Football_AI

Hand out to parents at orientation / first practice.
