Runner Guide · Five Looks, One Sentence

Follow. Plant. Go.

1. Tight Box (Inside Colors): Plant at the hole cone (C-RG or C-LG). Do not bounce early.
2. Off-Tackle Alley (Gold Star / Green Triangle): Follow lead to RG-RT or LG-LT alley. Plant, then north.
3. Wide Hip (Orange Square / Purple Hexagon): Landmark is RT or LT outside hip. Still Follow. Plant. Go.
4. Front Steps Up: Stay on the landmark cone. Do not dance. Plant once and drive through.
5. Empty Backside: Do not chase opposite grass if call is inside. The color is the hole!
Direct Snap ~3 Yards: Pictures change, words do not. No new plays on this card.

Laminate and keep inside coach clipboard or runner pouch.
