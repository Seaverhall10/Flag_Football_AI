Home Command Playbook Practice Drill Runner Print

Practice skeleton

Sixty minutes. Cart first. Same cues. Work the six runs; do not add a seventh.

Cart list

  • Football bag (size appropriate) + extra balls for 30-second next-ball
  • Flags / belts (check clips before first whistle)
  • Cones: holes C-RG, C-LG, RG-RT, LG-LT, RT outside, LT outside
  • Pinnies for 5v4 look
  • Water + first-aid + ice pack
  • Sideline card, tracker, whistle
  • Wristbands / flashcards if using stations

60 minutes

0–8
Arrival / flags / water. Cue posters on cones. No names on boards.
8–15
Warm movement. Find jersey. Head out. Hands inside. Light tags, not a race.
15–25
Snap station. Shotgun ~3 yards. Direct snap to designated runner only. Quiet count. Reset feet.
25–40
5v4 drill. 18-rep script. 30s next-ball. See drill page.
40–52
Six-run cycle. One color at a time. Landmark cone is the hole. Lead: hole first, LB.
52–58
Front / CB. Stay home, then flag. Nothing outside.
58–60
Close. Water. One cue repeat. Tracker marks. Parents: next time from calendar.

Stop rule sits on the clipboard. No new offense until all six average 5-of-6 clean snap, assignments, landmark, legal contact in two practices.
