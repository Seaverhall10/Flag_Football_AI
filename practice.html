Practice Timeline & Stations

60 Minutes Total
0–8m
Arrival, Flags & Equipment Setup

Check belt clips before first whistle. Cones on all 6 landmark holes. No player names on boards.

8–15m
Warm Movement & Fit-Up

Cues: Find jersey. Head out. Hands inside. Light tag drills, high knees, agility weave.

15–25m
Snap Station & Direct Exchange

Shotgun ~3 yards direct to designated runner. Focus on clean snap grip, quiet cadence, firm catch.

25–40m
5v4 Scripted Drill (18 Reps)

30-second next-ball tempo. 5 offense vs 4 look defense. Open 30s Play Clock →

40–52m
Six-Run Cycle

One color at a time. Landmark cones are the holes. Lead blocks hole first, then LB. Runner cuts north.

52–58m
Front & Cornerback Defense

Front 3: Stay home, then flag. Cornerbacks: Nothing outside, contain runner inside.

58–60m
Close, Water & Tracker Mark

Repeat the 5 cues together. Record clean reps on the tracker. Parents check schedule.
