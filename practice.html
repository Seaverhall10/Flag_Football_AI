Practice Timeline & Stations

60 Minutes Total
0–8m
Station 1: Arrival, Flags & Equipment Setup

Check belt clips. Cones on all 6 landmark holes. No player names on boards.

8–15m
Station 2: Warm Movement & Fit-Up

Cues: Find jersey. Head out. Hands inside. Light tag drills, agility weave.

15–25m
Station 3: Shotgun Snap Station

Shotgun ~3 yards direct to designated runner. Two-hand catch into chest.

25–40m
Station 4: 5v4 Scripted Drill (18 Reps)

30-sec next-ball tempo. 5 offense vs 4 look defense. Open 30s Play Clock →

40–52m
Station 5: Six-Run Cycle

One color at a time. Cones are the hole landmarks. Lead blocks hole first, LB.

52–58m
Station 6: Front 3 & CB Defense

Front stay home, then flag. Cornerbacks keep outside arm free, force runner inside.

58–60m
Station 7: Close & Team Review

Hydration, repeat 5 cues together, record clean reps on tracker clipboard.
