Five Cues That Never Change

One adult voice on the field
OL (Line) Find your jersey. Head out. Hands inside.
Lead Blocker Hole first. Then LB.
Runner Follow. Plant. Go.
Defensive Front Stay home, then flag.
Cornerback (CB) Nothing outside. Force in.
