5v4 drill

Eighteen reps. Thirty seconds to the next ball. Offense five, look-team four. Ages 5–6 pace.

Setup

  • Ball at shotgun ~3 yards. Direct snap to designated runner.
  • Cones on all six landmarks even if you only cycle three colors in a block.
  • Look-team: front stay home then flag; CB nothing outside. No live ripping at faces.
  • Coach is the clock. Next ball at 30s whether the last rep was pretty or not.
