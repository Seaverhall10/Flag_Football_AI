5v4 Scripted Drill

18 reps total. 30 seconds to the next ball. Teaching grid: 5 offense (OL + RB1/RB2/RB3) vs 4 defenders mapped from 8v8 (3 DL + LB, or 2 LB + 2 CB). High tempo, consistent cues.

Rep 1 of 18
30s
Inside Right · Red Diamond · C-RG
Key Focus: Snap + hole

Drill Setup & Rules of Engagement

  • Shotgun Snap: Ball placed ~3 yards back. Direct snap to designated runner only.
  • Cones in Ground: Place all 6 landmark cones (C-RG, C-LG, RG-RT, LG-LT, RT Out, LT Out) before starting.
  • Look Defense (4): Same 8v8 rules, smaller grid. Use 3 DL + 1 LB, or 2 LB + 2 CB. Front/DL stay home then flag. LB fills. CB nothing outside. No live tackling.
  • Coach is the Clock: Next ball snaps at 30s whether the prior rep was pretty or not. Keep the rhythm!
