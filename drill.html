5v4 Scripted Drill

18 reps total. 30 seconds to the next ball. 5 offensive players vs 4 look-team defenders. High tempo, fast reps, consistent cues.

Rep 1 of 18
30s
Inside Right · Red Diamond · C-RG
Key Focus: Snap + hole

Drill Setup & Rules of Engagement

  • Shotgun Snap: Ball placed ~3 yards back. Direct snap to designated runner only.
  • Cones in Ground: Place all 6 landmark cones (C-RG, C-LG, RG-RT, LG-LT, RT Out, LT Out) before starting.
  • Look Defense (4 Players): Front 3 stay home then flag; Cornerback keeps outside arm free, forces in. No live tackling or face rips.
  • Coach is the Clock: Next ball snaps at 30s whether the prior rep was pretty or not. Keep the rhythm!
