Five Runner Looks

The defensive picture changes. The words do not. Kids still hear three simple words: Follow. Plant. Go.

Defensive Looks & Runner Responses

1 · Tight Box (Clogged Middle)

Inside colors (Red Diamond / Blue Circle). Do not bounce outside early! Follow your lead blocker directly into the A-gap cone, plant your foot at the landmark, and accelerate north.

2 · Off-Tackle Alley (Off-Tackle Right/Left)

Gold Star / Green Triangle. Follow the lead blocker straight through the RG-RT or LG-LT gap. Plant on your outside foot as the lead makes contact, and cut upfield.

3 · Wide Hip (Perimeter Sweep)

Orange Square / Purple Hexagon. The landmark is the RT or LT outside hip cone. Trust your lead to seal the edge. Still: Follow. Plant. Go.

4 · Front Steps Up (Aggressive DL Rush)

Stay on the designated landmark hole cone. Do not dance or hesitate east-west. Plant once firmly and drive straight through the seam.

5 · Empty Backside (Over-Pursuit)

Do not chase open green grass on the opposite side if the call is inside. The color is the hole. Stick to the discipline of the play design.
