Home Command Playbook Practice Drill Runner Print

Five looks

The picture changes. The words do not. Kids still hear Follow. Plant. Go.

1 · Tight box. Inside colors. Plant at the hole cone, then go. Do not bounce early.
2 · Off-tackle alley. Gold star / green triangle. Follow the lead to RG-RT or LG-LT. Plant, then north.
3 · Wide hip. Orange square / purple hexagon. Landmark is RT or LT outside hip. Still Follow. Plant. Go.
4 · Front steps up. Stay on the landmark. Do not dance. Plant once. Go.
5 · Empty backside. Do not chase grass if the call is inside. The color is the hole.

Call language

Inside Right Red Diamond · hole C-RG
Inside Left Blue Circle · hole C-LG
Off-Tackle Right Gold Star · hole RG-RT
Off-Tackle Left Green Triangle · hole LG-LT
Wide Right Orange Square · hole RT outside hip
Wide Left Purple Hexagon · hole LT outside hip

Same three words on every color. New looks are pictures, not new plays.
