Coach the rep
1

Point before the snap

Center points to the Linebacker. Guard and Tackle point to their D-line jobs. The Lead RB points to the lane or outside Linebacker.

2

Center to Linebacker

Snap, take one clean angle to the Linebacker's starting landmark, fit with head out and open hands inside, then use short controlled steps. Stop balanced when the Linebacker sheds.

3

Outside run

Runner reaches the Tackle's outside hip before turning north. Corner stays unblocked and keeps outside leverage.

4

Fit and shed safely

Two-point stance. Eyes up. Head out. Open hands inside the legal torso. Controlled feet. Whistle and reset any unsafe balance, head position, or hands.
