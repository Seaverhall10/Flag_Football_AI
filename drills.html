Station 1: Arrival & Equipment Setup

Check player belt clips and flag tightness. Place 6 landmark cones at exact hole locations. No player names on boards.

Focus: Safety & Equipment Check

Station 2: Warm Movement & Fit-Up

Line up in 3-point stance. Call out cues: Find jersey. Head out. Hands inside. Agility cone weave with 2-hand flag pulls.

Focus: Stance, Hands & Flag Pull
15–25 min

Station 3: Shotgun Snap Station

Centers and Runners in pairs at 3-yard depth. Direct snap into chest. 10 clean reps per designated runner.

Focus: Direct Snap & Two-Hand Catch
25–40 min

Station 4: 18-Rep 5v4 Tempo Drill

30-second next-ball pace. 5 offensive players vs 4 look defenders. Coach feeds ball instantly at whistle. Snap + assignment + landmark.

Focus: Fast Huddle & 30s Tempo
40–52 min

Station 5: Six-Run Landmark Cycle

Full 8v8 execution cycling through all 6 runs. Lead blocker attacks called hole first, seals Linebacker. Runner follows lead and cuts north.

Focus: 8-Man Linemen Blocking Angles
52–58 min

Station 6: Defensive Front & CB Contain

Front 3: Stay home, then flag (no overpursuing). Cornerbacks: Nothing outside (force runner inside to pursuit).

Focus: Containment & Gap Discipline
58–60 min

Station 7: Team Huddle & 5-of-6 Tracker

Water break, team cheer (1-2-3 LIONS!), and record clean reps in Notes tracker to certify Stop Rule completion.

Focus: Culture & Stop Rule Certification
