1

Point Before the Snap

Each blocker points to one defender. Each defender points to the lane or leverage they own. If two kids point to the same job, reset before contact.

2

Inside Run

Runner presses between Center and Guard. Lead RB enters first. Defense squeezes the lane without abandoning outside leverage.

3

Outside Run

Runner reaches the Tackle's outside hip before turning north. Corner stays outside; pursuit works inside-out.

4

Block and Shed Safely

Two-point stance, eyes up, head out, open hands inside. Defender anchors, separates, finds the ball, and sheds toward the lane—never through a child's head or neck.

6. Score the Play, Not the Child

Clean snap: Center reaches the Runner's chest; Runner secures it before moving.
Correct targets: All three blockers identify the right defender before the snap.
Legal fit: Head out, hands inside, feet under control, no holding or takedown.
Lane integrity: DL protects the assigned gap, LB stays square, CB keeps outside leverage.
Finish: Offense reaches the lane; defense separates, pursues, and pulls the flag.
